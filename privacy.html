Spend Ledger

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 16, 2026

What Spend Ledger is

Spend Ledger is a personal expense tracker. You sign in with your Google account to keep your own private ledger, and can optionally connect your Gmail account so the app can automatically detect bank, card, and OTT subscription transaction emails and turn them into expense entries for you to review.

Information we collect

  • Account info: when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture to create your account and identify you within the app.
  • Gmail data (only if you connect Gmail): with your explicit consent, we request read-only (gmail.readonly) access to your Gmail. We use it to run a narrow, specific search for emails that look like bank/card transaction alerts or OTT receipts (e.g. from known bank and merchant senders) — we do not browse your inbox generally, and we never send, delete, label, or modify any email. For each matched email we extract the amount, a category guess, the date, and a short snippet of the relevant text so you can review it before it's added to your ledger.
  • Expense & budget data: expenses you enter manually, expenses imported from Gmail (once you confirm them), per-category monthly budgets, and push-notification subscription details if you enable budget alerts.

How we use your information

Solely to operate Spend Ledger for you: authenticate you, show your own expenses and budgets, parse transaction emails into pending entries for your review, and send you a push notification if a category goes over its budget. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build any kind of profile beyond what's needed to run the app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Your data is never used to train AI/ML models. The only external service involved is Google, for sign-in and (if you opt in) reading matching Gmail messages — Spend Ledger's own servers and database are the only place your data is stored.

Data storage & security

Your data is stored in a private database associated with this app. Gmail OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. Each user's data is isolated — one account cannot see another account's expenses, budgets, or Gmail connection.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can disconnect Gmail access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops any further email access. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at the address below — we'll delete it within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Spend Ledger is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
